# Nonfunctional requirements

| Requirement | User impact | Target | Measurement setup | Owner | Acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unfilled | Decide from real journeys | No default target | Workload/device/network | Assign | Not measured |

Consider latency percentiles, throughput, availability, recovery time, tolerable data loss, accessibility, offline behavior, retention and cost. Choose targets with the project owner. Do not inherit arbitrary enterprise targets from inspiration or a knowledge base.
